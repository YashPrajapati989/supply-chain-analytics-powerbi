# Supply Chain Analytics — Power BI

## 📊 Project Overview

An end-to-end Supply Chain Analytics solution designed to provide
management with visibility into business performance, procurement,
supplier reliability, inventory health and logistics performance.

The solution combines SQL data analysis, Power BI data modelling,
DAX measures and executive dashboard design to transform operational
data into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Monitor overall business performance
- Analyse procurement spend by supplier
- Identify supplier delivery risks
- Monitor inventory health and stock shortages
- Analyse logistics and delivery performance
- Identify products requiring replenishment
- Provide management with actionable recommendations

## 📈 Key Business Findings

### Delivery Reliability

On-time delivery is currently **56.57%**, significantly below the
90% target.

### Supplier Risk

Several suppliers combine significant procurement spend with weak
delivery performance.

### Inventory Risk

Multiple products require replenishment, creating potential stockout
risk.

### Logistics

A significant number of shipments are being delivered late,
requiring supplier and logistics intervention.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

1. Improve supplier delivery reliability through corrective-action plans.
2. Review supplier spend allocation where high spend is combined with
   poor delivery performance.
3. Prioritise replenishment of the largest inventory shortages.
4. Monitor logistics performance against the 90% on-time target.
5. Establish recurring supplier performance reviews.
