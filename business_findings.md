# Business Findings

## Executive summary

The analysis indicates that the most material issue is **delivery reliability**, not simply procurement cost.

### Finding 1 — Service reliability is below target

- On-time delivery: **56.57%**
- Target: **90%**
- Gap: **33.43 percentage points**
- Late shipments: **304**

This creates a direct customer-service and planning risk.

### Finding 2 — Supplier performance is uneven

The supplier dashboard identifies weak delivery performers that should be managed through corrective-action plans.

The most visible high-risk combination is:

- weak on-time delivery
- material procurement spend
- recurring delivery delays

Metro IT Supply is particularly important because it combines the highest displayed procurement spend with weak delivery performance.

### Finding 3 — Inventory needs focused replenishment

- Current stock units: approximately **3.4K**
- Products requiring reorder: **11**
- At-risk inventory value: approximately **£93K**
- Average stock coverage: approximately **18.26 days**

The correct management response is not blanket inventory growth. Prioritise the largest shortfalls and critical/high-priority products.

### Finding 4 — Logistics needs root-cause management

The logistics dashboard records:

- **700 shipments**
- **304 late shipments**
- **43.43% late delivery**

Management should evaluate promised vs actual delivery days, carrier reliability and transport cost together.

### Finding 5 — Procurement concentration deserves attention

Total procurement spend is approximately **£3.20M**. High spend with a poorly performing supplier increases operational dependency and should trigger sourcing / allocation review.
