# Management Recommendations

## Priority 1 — Supplier recovery programme

**Why:** OTD is only 56.57% against a 90% target.

**Action:**
- Create corrective-action plans for the weakest suppliers.
- Set weekly OTD checkpoints.
- Require root-cause analysis for recurring delays.
- Escalate suppliers that do not improve within the agreed recovery period.

**Success measure:** supplier OTD moves toward the 90% target.

---

## Priority 2 — Protect availability through targeted replenishment

**Why:** 11 products require reorder and at-risk inventory is approximately £93K.

**Action:**
- Prioritise the Top 5 replenishment gaps.
- Review reorder points and safety stock for critical items.
- Introduce exception alerts rather than increasing all inventory levels.

**Success measure:** fewer reorder exceptions and reduced stockout exposure.

---

## Priority 3 — Reduce supplier dependency

**Why:** A high-spend supplier with weak delivery performance creates concentration risk.

**Action:**
- Benchmark alternative suppliers.
- Rebalance future purchase orders where service levels remain below target.
- Use supplier scorecards in sourcing decisions.

**Success measure:** lower dependency on weak suppliers without increasing unit cost materially.

---

## Priority 4 — Improve logistics execution

**Why:** 304 shipments were late.

**Action:**
- Compare promised vs actual days by carrier.
- Investigate recurring late routes / warehouses.
- Review carrier SLAs and service credits.
- Balance delivery reliability against transport cost.

**Success measure:** lower late-shipment count and a sustained improvement in OTD.

---

## Priority 5 — Establish executive governance

Create a monthly management scorecard containing:

1. On-Time Delivery %
2. Late Shipments
3. Products Requiring Reorder
4. At-Risk Inventory Value
5. Supplier Risk
6. Procurement Spend

Use a simple status system:

- 🟢 On target / healthy
- 🟠 Monitor
- 🔴 Immediate action

The purpose is to make the dashboard a decision-support tool rather than a reporting-only artifact.
